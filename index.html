What is NTS?

NTS captures real language situations from your daily life. Every situation becomes reusable learning material.

When should I save an NTS?

When you hear a useful sentence, a new phrase, something someone says, or something you wanted to say.

📝 Useful sentence 🗣️ New phrase 👂 Someone said 💭 Wanted to say
What happens after saving?

1. Save → 2. Organize → 3. Review → 4. Learn naturally

What is Veseh?

Veseh is your personal phrase library. It stores expressions grouped by meaning instead of grammar.

Why ChatGPT?

Use it for: correcting mistakes, conversation practice, and explanation. Do not use it as permanent storage.

Why DeepSeek?

Use it for: analysis, comparing phrases, and translation. Not for everyday storage.

Tips
Don't save single words
Save complete situations

Quick FAQ
Can I edit later?

Yes. Everything can be edited at any time.

Can I delete entries?

Yes. Remove what you no longer need.

Should I translate everything?

Only translate what you need to say.

How many NTS per day?

1–3 is perfect. Quality matters more than quantity.
